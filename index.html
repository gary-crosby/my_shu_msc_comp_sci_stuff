  • Week 4: Discussion 4.7

    Adding a little JavaScript to my 'Hello World' page felt mundane. So, for funsies, I linked to a simple game (demo) I built using JavaScript: Asteroid Alert!. I created this project in early 2026 as an example for the students in the Creative Computing (MULT 217) module I lead at Saskatchewan Polytechnic. Its primary purpose was to give the students an idea of the scope of their final project. Its secondary purpose was to relieve the sense of general boredom I was feeling at the time :-) The game isn't a responsive design, it could benefit from improved gameplay, and the JavaScript is a sloppy mix of procedural and OOP styles. But it works well enough for its intended purpose. It's about 900 lines of my JavaScript (plus HTML and CSS), it leverages the wonderful p5.js (an open source JavaScript library) and it uses a handful of publicly available media assets. For source code, credits and additional information, see GitHub repo. I hope you enjoy playing it!
